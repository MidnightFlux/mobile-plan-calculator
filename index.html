How this is calculated

people = sub-lines + 1 (the main line is a person too). Sub-lines can be 0–3.

1 · Contract total

Main plan= plan cost × months
Sub-lines= cost per sub-line × sub-lines × months
Phone deal= contract price − selling price(negative = profit)
Staff rebate= annual rebate × months ÷ 12
Total= the four lines above, added up

2 · Split across people

Rebate sharedeach person = total ÷ people
Rebate to the main lineequal share = total before rebate ÷ people
main line = equal share − staff rebate
each sub-line = equal share

3 · Per month

Any amount= the whole-term amount ÷ months

The phone retail price is reference only: it shows the resale value of the deal, but it is not part of the split. The split uses what you actually paid (contract price) against what you actually sold it for (selling price).

With "rebate applies to the main line only" switched on, the service and phone costs are still split evenly and the whole rebate comes off the main line's share — so the group total is unchanged, but the main line can end up paying less than nothing if the rebate is bigger than its share.
