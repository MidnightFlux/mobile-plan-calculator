How this is calculated

people = sub-lines + 1 (the main line is a person too).

monthly service = plan cost + (cost per sub-line × sub-lines) rebate total = annual rebate × months ÷ 12 phone net = contract price − selling price (negative = profit) total cost = monthly service × months − rebate total + phone net per person/mo = total cost ÷ months ÷ people

The phone retail price is reference only: it shows the resale value of the deal, but it is not part of the split. The split uses what you actually paid (contract price) against what you actually sold it for (selling price).
